How it works

Local direction before outside participation

A useful partnership begins with the problem as communities understand it, then matches the right people, resources, safeguards, and evidence to the work.

01
Community direction

Priorities are identified with communities and local partners before participant or funder activity is designed.

02
Right role, right support

People are matched to work that fits their skills, preparation, safeguarding requirements, and the supervision available.

03
Useful work and shared learning

Each engagement should produce value for the community as well as learning for the participant or institution.

04
Evidence with consent

FOSCOD separates verified results from future targets and publishes personal stories only when consent is recorded.

Featured impact story

Clean energy that powers a livelihood

— Community voice: Sarah N., Naluvule Village

"The solar salon transformed my business. Before, I closed every evening when the power went out. Now I serve clients until 10pm."

In Naluvule Village, a solar-powered hair salon turned an unreliable income into a growing business. Sarah Namubiru invested her first profit back into a second solar system for a neighbor, creating a small energy-sharing network.

The project is verified through FOSCOD's MEL framework — energy access, income tracking, and community adoption all measured quarterly.
